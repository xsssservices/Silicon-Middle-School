SMART Goals

These goals are specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-based so they clearly guide the planning and success of the Play Day.

1. Increase Student Participation

Specific: Students will take part in at least four activity stations.
Measurable: At least 90% of students will complete the rotation.
Achievable: Stations will be organized and supervised.
Relevant: This supports active participation and enjoyment.
Time-based: This will be completed during the Play Day event.

2. Improve Teamwork and Inclusion

Specific: Students will work in mixed groups during cooperative tasks.
Measurable: Teachers will observe that each group completes at least one team challenge.
Achievable: Group tasks will be designed for different skill levels.
Relevant: This supports belonging, cooperation, and confidence.
Time-based: This will be achieved throughout the event day.

3. Build Leadership Skills

Specific: I will lead the event by organizing stations and encouraging students.
Measurable: I will complete a leadership checklist during the event.
Achievable: I will prepare a clear schedule and roles in advance.
Relevant: This supports strong event leadership and student support.
Time-based: This will be demonstrated during the planning and execution of Play Day.
