Needs Assessment

Purpose

Before planning Silicon Middle School Play Day, Vee's Events conducted a student needs assessment to learn what students want, what they need, and how confident they feel trying new activities. These responses helped the team design activities that are engaging, inclusive, and suited to different skill levels.

Student Survey Questions

A survey was given to 240 students at Silicon Middle School before the event. The questions were designed to gather information about student interests, current skill levels, confidence, and support needs so the event could be planned around student voice.

  • Rarely

4. What helps you feel included and supported during physical activities?

  • Working with friends

  • Music and entertainment

How It Affects Our Play Day

Based on the survey results, Vee's Events will focus on creating a balance between competitive and non-competitive activities. Since many students enjoy teamwork and social activities, the event will include cooperative challenges, relay activities, and group games. Because some students are less confident trying new activities, stations will be designed with different difficulty levels so everyone can participate comfortably.

The results also show that students value inclusion, which supports our goal of creating a Play Day where every student feels welcomed and successful.
