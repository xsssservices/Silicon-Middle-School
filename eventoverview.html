Event Overview

Silicon Middle School Play Day is a student-centered event that combines fun, physical activity, teamwork, and wellness into one exciting school experience.

Purpose of the Event

The event is designed to encourage students to be active, build friendships, and improve confidence through movement-based activities that are inclusive and enjoyable for everyone.

Event Details

The Play Day will take place at the school field and gymnasium, with stations focused on sports, teamwork, leadership, and healthy living. Students will rotate through activities in a structured and supportive environment.

What Students Will Experience

  • Engaging physical activities and team challenges
  • Opportunities to try new games and build confidence
  • A welcoming atmosphere that supports inclusion and participation
  • Moments for reflection, teamwork, and positive peer interaction

Why It Matters

This event helps students connect movement with well-being, leadership, and community spirit. It creates a memorable day that promotes healthy habits while making school life more exciting and meaningful.
