Survey Summary

The exit survey showed that students had a very positive experience during Silicon Middle School Play Day. The results suggest that the event was engaging, welcoming, and successful in helping students enjoy physical activity and teamwork.

Strength: A major strength was the high level of student enjoyment, with 220 out of 240 students saying they enjoyed the activities. In addition, 210 out of 240 students felt included and supported, which shows the event created a positive and welcoming environment.
Room for Improvement: Some students asked for more activity choices and more breaks throughout the day. This feedback is useful because it shows that while the event was successful, future Play Days could be even better by offering more variety and giving students more time to rest and recharge between stations.

Overall conclusion: The survey data shows that the event met the needs of most students and provided a fun, engaging experience. The results also give clear guidance for improvement in future events.
