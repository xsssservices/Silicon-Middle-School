Action Plan

1. Event Overview

Silicon Middle School Play Day will be hosted at Silicon Middle School from 9:00 AM - 3:00 PM.

The event will welcome 240 students divided into 12 groups of 20 students. Each group will rotate through carefully planned stations that balance fun fitness, teamwork, leadership, wellness, and inclusion.

This action plan is designed to create a smooth, engaging, and highly organized day where every student can participate, learn, and enjoy themselves.

2. Staff & Responsibilities

Event Coordinator

Me - Head of Vee's Events

  • Create the full event schedule and station flow
  • Communicate with teachers and staff before the event
  • Oversee all activity stations and transitions
  • Lead problem-solving during the day
  • Ensure safety procedures and student support

Teachers/Supervisors

  • Supervise student groups and maintain order
  • Support activity leaders during rotations
  • Monitor student behaviour and participation
  • Help with movement between stations
  • Report concerns to the event coordinator

Activity Leaders

  • Explain game rules and expectations clearly
  • Encourage all students to participate
  • Adapt activities for different confidence levels
  • Keep energy high and students engaged
  • Provide encouragement and feedback throughout the day

Safety & Wellness Team

  • Handle minor injuries and respond quickly
  • Monitor hydration and student wellness
  • Check equipment before each rotation
  • Support inclusive participation and comfort
  • Manage the wellness and recovery area

3. Planning Before the Event

6 Weeks Before

  • Confirm the date, school location, and event schedule
  • Review student survey results and activity preferences
  • Select unique workshops and stations that match student interests
  • Create a budget, supply list, and equipment checklist
  • Assign staff roles and confirm teacher support

4 Weeks Before

  • Reserve all equipment needed for the stations
  • Create the student group rotations and station order
  • Plan lunch, hydration breaks, and wellness support
  • Prepare safety procedures and emergency contacts
  • Train volunteers and leaders on activity expectations

2 Weeks Before

  • Complete a final equipment and space check
  • Confirm staff and volunteer attendance
  • Print schedules, station cards, and student group lists
  • Prepare signage and clear instructions for each station
  • Finalize the day flow and transition plans

1 Day Before

  • Set up all equipment and station areas
  • Organize materials by location and rotation
  • Review the full schedule with staff and volunteers
  • Test the safety equipment and first-aid supplies
  • Make sure every area is ready for a smooth start

4. Event Day Flow (9:00 AM - 3:00 PM)

Time Activity Purpose
9:00 - 9:20 Arrival, attendance, and opening welcome Sets a positive tone and introduces the theme of movement, teamwork, and fun
9:20 - 10:00 Workshop Rotation 1 Students begin with energetic activities that build confidence and excitement
10:05 - 10:45 Workshop Rotation 2 Students rotate to a different station to keep the day active and varied
10:50 - 11:30 Workshop Rotation 3 Students experience leadership and cooperation activities in a structured way
11:30 - 12:15 Lunch and wellness break Provides rest, hydration, and a reset before the second half of the day
12:15 - 12:55 Workshop Rotation 4 Students continue building skills through active and engaging challenges
1:00 - 1:40 Workshop Rotation 5 Students try a new activity or challenge that keeps the day fun and exciting
1:45 - 2:30 Team Challenge Finale Students work together in a final movement-based challenge that celebrates teamwork
2:30 - 3:00 Reflection, awards, and dismissal Ends the day with recognition, positive feedback, and a sense of accomplishment

5. Unique Workshops, Activities, and Fun Fitness

Fun Fitness Circuit

Includes:

  • Relay races
  • Agility cones and obstacle runs
  • Balance and coordination games

This station keeps students active while building energy, confidence, and physical literacy.

Leadership and Team Challenge Zone

Includes:

  • Communication challenges
  • Partner problem-solving tasks
  • Group puzzle and strategy games

This station develops teamwork, leadership, and shared responsibility.

Sports Skill Lab

Includes:

  • Basketball shooting drills
  • Soccer ball control activities
  • Volleyball passing and teamwork games

This station helps students build skills while enjoying friendly competition.

Wellness and Mindful Movement

Includes:

  • Stretching and mobility activities
  • Breathing and mindfulness exercises
  • Low-pressure movement challenges

This station supports mental wellness and helps students feel calm and included.

Creative Movement and Dance

Includes:

  • Music-based movement games
  • Rhythm and coordination activities
  • Group choreography challenges

This station adds a fun and energetic element that appeals to different personalities and interests.

Final Challenge Arena

Includes:

  • Mini team relay races
  • Cooperative problem-solving tasks
  • Celebration and award moments

This closing activity brings the whole day together and reinforces teamwork, effort, and success.

6. Organization and Flow of the Day

The day is organized with a strong flow so students always know where they are going and what to expect. Students move in assigned groups, each station is clearly explained, and transitions are planned to avoid confusion and wasted time.

  • Groups rotate on a timed schedule to keep the day moving smoothly
  • Each station has clear instructions, leaders, and expectations
  • Water breaks and wellness time are built into the schedule
  • Students experience a balance of energetic, skill-based, team-based, and reflective activities
  • The structure helps create a positive atmosphere where students feel supported and engaged

7. Safety, Equipment, and Food Plan

Safety Measures

  • Students stay with assigned groups throughout the day
  • Staff supervise each station and transition point
  • Equipment is checked before each rotation
  • Water breaks happen regularly to prevent dehydration
  • First aid supplies and emergency procedures are available at all times
  • Activities are adapted to support students of different abilities and confidence levels

Food & Wellness Plan

Lunch (11:30 AM - 12:15 PM):

  • Healthy meal options will be provided
  • Water stations will be available throughout the day
  • Snack breaks and rest time are included
  • Allergies and dietary needs will be considered

Wellness Focus:

  • Keep students energized and comfortable
  • Encourage healthy choices and positive habits
  • Support both physical and mental well-being

8. After the Event

After Play Day, Vee's Events will:

  • Collect exit survey results from students
  • Review feedback from students, teachers, and volunteers
  • Discuss what went well and what could be improved
  • Identify strengths in leadership, organization, and station design
  • Document lessons learned for future events
  • Create recommendations that will improve the next Play Day experience

This reflection step ensures the event continues to grow and become even more effective in future years.
