Brainstorm

Based on the results from the student needs assessment, Vees's Events brainstormed ideas to create an engaging, inclusive and memorable Play Day. This brainstorming section includes themes, important considerations, and activity ideas that were chosen to reflect student interests, support different skill levels, and make the event meaningful.

Theme Ideas

Move Together, Thrive Together

Focus: Teamwork, inclusion, and school spirit.

This theme emphasizes collaboration and belonging, creating an environment where every student feels valued and connected. Activities focus on building community and working toward shared goals.

Strong Bodies, Strong Minds

Focus: Physical activity and mental wellness.

This theme connects physical fitness with mental and emotional well-being. Students explore how movement benefits both their bodies and minds while developing healthy habits.

Challenge Beyond Limits

Focus: Personal growth and trying new activities.

This theme encourages students to step outside their comfort zones and discover new capabilities. Activities are designed to build confidence and showcase that challenges lead to growth.

Important Considerations

Safety

  • First aid station
  • Water/hydration areas
  • Activity rules
  • Staff supervision

Inclusion

  • Adapted games
  • Different difficulty levels
  • Non-competitive options

Organization

  • Student groups
  • Equipment setup
  • Volunteer roles
  • Schedule planning

Student Feedback

  • Include activities students requested
  • Balance competition and cooperation
  • Provide breaks

Activities

Activities were chosen from the survey results to ensure they match student interests and support our Play Day goals.

Team Sports

Examples:

  • Soccer
  • Basketball
  • Volleyball

Reason: Team sports were one of the most popular choices in the student survey. These activities promote teamwork, communication, and friendly competition while keeping students physically active.

Obstacle Course

Examples:

  • Agility challenges
  • Relay races
  • Balance stations

Reason: Obstacle courses were highly requested in the student survey. They help improve coordination, movement skills, confidence, and problem-solving.

Cooperative Challenges

Examples:

  • Team puzzles
  • Communication games
  • Group challenges

Reason: These activities encourage teamwork, communication, and inclusion by allowing students with different skill levels to contribute.

Wellness Station

Examples:

  • Stretching area
  • Mindfulness activities
  • Relaxation station

Reason: Wellness activities support mental health and provide students with a balance between physical challenges and relaxation.

Final Theme Selection

Move Together, Thrive Together

Reason: This theme was selected because it represents the main goal of our Play Day: creating a welcoming environment where every student can participate, build confidence, and enjoy physical activity with their peers.
